## Doctrine interpretation

1. **Ideas #1–#3 live in the +5σ–10σ band → deep_review territory.**
   These are the *category-shaping* candidates the doctrine asks for;
   they need Mike-gated review before any external commitment.

2. **Ideas #4–#7 live in the +3σ–5σ band → collision territory.**
   These are *differentiated* approaches: the doctrine's Collision
   Engine should be applied — pair them, fuse elements, run another
   repair cycle.

3. **Ideas #8–#9 live in the 0σ–3σ band → anti_median territory.**
   They are *commodity* (the engine itself is the commodity, the
   differentiation must come from packaging). The doctrine recommends
   routing to the Anti-Median Engine for re-scoping.

4. **No idea hit the wall.** This is good — it means none of the
   candidates are pathological outliers or numerical artifacts. All
   the z values are real deviations, not numerical explosions.

5. **Invention-repaired-tail is the consistent weak point.** 6 of 9
   ideas have invention-repaired-z = 0.5 (the default coherence floor)
   because the IdeaGenome anti-unification found an empty shared
   skeleton G. To clear this: the idea's core must include a
   *mechanism* (not just an offer). Doctrine-to-Deal's "assessment"
   *is* a mechanism; OaaS's "retainer" is not.

1. **Week 1–4: Doctrine-to-Deal (#1).** Free funnel, $1K deposit gate.
   The repair shows it survives the negative-tail crash; the only

   assessment tells you who is real; the retainer tells you whether

4. **Week 8–12: Operator Studio (#4) for proof accumulation.** Sign

5. **Day 90: Re-run Deviatrix.** The doctrine's 90-day falsifier cycle.
